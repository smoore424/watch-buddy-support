
# WatchBuddy Support

WatchBuddy is a free iPhone and iPad app for keeping track of the TV shows you're watching. Find shows, add them to My Shows, and check off episodes as you watch them, so you always know where you left off.

## Contact

Have a question, a problem, or an idea? Email **[watchbuddyapp@gmail.com](mailto:watchbuddyapp@gmail.com)**.

You can also email from inside the app: go to **Settings → Get in Touch → Contact**.

If you're reporting a problem, it helps to include the app version (shown in **Settings → Version**) and what you were doing when it happened.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add a show?**
Find the show on the **Discover** tab, by browsing or searching, and tap the **+** button.

**How do I mark an episode as watched?**
Go to **My Shows**, tap the show, choose a season, and tap an episode to check it off. To mark a whole season at once, tap the circle next to the season name at the top of the episode list.

**How do I remove a show?**
On **My Shows**, press and hold the show and choose **Remove from List**. This also removes its watch progress.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's nothing to sign up for.

**Is my information private?**
Yes. Your shows and watch progress are stored only on your device, and WatchBuddy doesn't collect any personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**Where does the show information come from?**
Show details, episodes, and posters come from [The Movie Database (TMDB)](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. "Where to Watch" streaming information is provided by JustWatch through TMDB and currently shows availability in the United States.

**Does WatchBuddy cost anything?**
No. WatchBuddy is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
