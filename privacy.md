# WatchBuddy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 29, 2026

WatchBuddy is a free app for keeping track of the TV shows you watch. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short, **WatchBuddy does not collect any of your personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. WatchBuddy:

- does not require an account, sign-in, name, or email address
- does not include analytics, crash reporting, or advertising
- does not track you across other apps or websites
- does not sell or share your information with anyone

The developer has no server and cannot see which shows you add or which episodes you watch.

## What is stored on your device

To work, WatchBuddy saves the following **only on your device**:

- the shows you add to My Shows
- which episodes you have marked as watched
- your app settings, such as Light/Dark mode and the last tab you used

This information is not sent to the developer. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, Apple may include this app data in your device backup, according to your own device settings.

## Show information from TMDB

WatchBuddy gets show details, episode lists, posters, and "Where to Watch" information from [The Movie Database (TMDB)](https://www.themoviedb.org). To do this, the app connects directly to TMDB over the internet when you browse, search, or open a show.

These requests contain only what TMDB needs to return results, such as the words you search for or the ID of the show you opened. They do not include your name or any account information. As with any website or online service, TMDB can see the IP address the request comes from. TMDB's handling of that information is covered by the [TMDB Privacy Policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

"Where to Watch" streaming information is provided to TMDB by JustWatch. WatchBuddy does not connect to JustWatch directly.

WatchBuddy uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

- To remove a single show and its watch progress, remove it from My Shows.
- To remove everything, delete the WatchBuddy app from your device.

## Children's privacy

WatchBuddy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of WatchBuddy adds features that handle your information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
